Coins that launch
when they are funded

Set a goal in ETH and a window. Backers put ETH in. The moment the goal is met, anyone can press launch: the coin goes live on Pons, half of the raise buys the first coins for the backers, the other half becomes a chest that buys and burns whenever the curve drops below where it opened. Miss the goal and everyone takes their ETH back.
