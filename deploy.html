Deploy Pledge

Two contracts on Robinhood Chain. The wallet that deploys the factory becomes the guardian: it can propose a new burner with a 48 hour notice and nothing else.

  1. Deploy the pledge implementation, every pledge is a clone of it
  2. Deploy the burner (the same TipBurner-style contract is not part of this package: use any address that will hold and burn $PLEDGE, or the treasury wallet for now)
  3. Deploy the factory with the implementation and the burner
  4. Put the factory and the burner into config.js and push

1 · Pledge implementation

No constructor, no owner. It is only a template, locked on deploy

2 · Burner address

Receives a tenth of every collected fee. Can be rotated later by the guardian with a 48 hour notice
